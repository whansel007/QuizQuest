# Phone checklist (about 10 minutes)

Setup: `pnpm build` then `pnpm start` on the computer; the phone on the same Wi-Fi opens `http://<computer-IP>:3000`. Sign in as `student01@quizquest.demo` / `quizquest`. A second phone or a desktop tab as `student02@quizquest.demo` helps with steps 13-14 and 20.

Tick each box; note anything odd next to it.

## Sign in and navigation (portrait)

- [ ] 1. **Sign in**: the sign-in page fits the screen with no sideways scroll; tapping the email field does not zoom the page; Continue → password → Sign in works.
- [ ] 2. **Nav drawer**: on the Quiz page, tap ☰ (top right). The drawer slides in with the five pages, your name, Log out, Tools and sound. Close it by tapping outside, then open it again and close it with ✕.
- [ ] 3. **Immersive pages**: open Kingdom from the drawer. Only a small ☰ button sits in the top-right corner; it opens the drawer in one tap.

## Quiz

- [ ] 4. **Start a quiz**: Quiz → Start practice. The question and the answer cards fit the width; each card is easy to tap.
- [ ] 5. **Answer**: tap an answer (or type a written/numeric answer: the keyboard must not cover the Submit button). The result block appears; Next question works.
- [ ] 6. **Timer**: start a timed quiz (Timer: 30 s). Scroll the question: the countdown pill stays pinned top right.

## Kingdom

- [ ] 7. **Walk**: tap the ground; the avatar walks there and the camera follows.
- [ ] 8. **Pinch**: pinch the town to zoom in and out (whole town to close-up). Turn on the 🕹️ stick (bottom left) and walk with it; turn it off again.
- [ ] 9. **Upgrade**: tap a building door bubble (or Buildings → a building). The panel opens full screen; the close button and the Upgrade/Build button are reachable without scrolling.
- [ ] 10. **Avatar colour**: open the Castle → wardrobe → Colors, pick a colour and save; the avatar changes colour in town.

## World (Campfire Run select)

- [ ] 11. **Select**: open Campfire Run. Scroll the page: room code box, Camp Buffs, hero stats, campfire scene and hero list all show; tap a different hero and the preview changes.
- [ ] 12. **Create lobby**: tap HOST LOBBY. The game opens; the site nav is gone.
- [ ] 13. **Join with code**: on the second device, type the room code from the first (top right of the game) and tap JOIN; both heroes appear.

## In-run (landscape)

- [ ] 14. **Rotate**: in portrait the game shows "Rotate your phone to play"; turn the phone sideways and it disappears. The HUD is compact: party top left, wave/campfire top centre, room code + ☰ top right.
- [ ] 15. **Move**: put your left thumb anywhere on the left side of the screen and drag; the stick appears under the thumb and the hero moves (8 directions).
- [ ] 16. **Tap to cast**: tap FIRE (M1) and then SKILL (E): each fires at the nearest enemy or dummy (auto-aim).
- [ ] 17. **Aim a direction ability**: hold SKILL, drag toward a target (an aim line appears from the hero), release: it fires that way.
- [ ] 18. **Cancel**: hold ULT, drag out, then drag back onto the button (it turns red) and release: nothing happens and the cooldown is not spent.
- [ ] 19. **Hold fire**: hold FIRE and drag around: the hero keeps firing and follows your thumb. (As Ranger: hold to charge, release to shoot.)
- [ ] 20. **Point ability**: as Engineer, hold SKILL and drag a short way, release: the turret lands at the reticle (never further than its range). As Ranger the meat works the same way.
- [ ] 21. **Bombardier targeting**: as Bombardier, tap ULT: a Cancel button with a draining ring appears; tap the arena: the strike lands there. Next time, hold ULT, drag the reticle onto enemies and release. Once more, open it and tap Cancel: no strike, no cooldown.
- [ ] 22. **Emote**: tap 👋 (top-left of the buttons), pick an emoji: it pops above your hero on both devices.
- [ ] 23. **Pinch**: pinch the arena with two fingers on the right half: the view zooms in and out but never shows outside the arena; the controls keep working. ☰ → Settings → Reset zoom restores it.
- [ ] 24. **Menu and Settings**: ☰ → Settings (Touch controls, Move stick, Vibrate, Reset zoom, effects), close; ☰ → Information shows the stats and the mobile help; ☰ → Fullscreen (Android) fills the screen; ☰ → Leave room returns to the Kingdom.

## Summon

- [ ] 25. **Summon x1 and x10, History**: open Summon. Pull once, then ten times: the 10-pull results show 2 per row in portrait and 5 per row in landscape. Open History: it fills the screen with a sticky header; switch tabs and close it.
