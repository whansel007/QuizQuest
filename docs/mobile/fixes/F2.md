# F2: town hitbox bug (avatar cannot walk upward on the path)

Branch: worktree branch off `mobile-fixes` (a00488c). Commits prefixed `fix(F2)`.

## Reproduction

412 x 915, touch emulation, `/?debug=colliders`. Walking the avatar (keys or the stick) to open grass just right of the trail beside the BATTLES sign (x ≈ 441, y ≈ 200) and holding up, it stops dead at **(437.7..440.7, 40.7)**, the foot of the north tree line, with open grass and no drawing above it. The same happens at every viewport (375 x 667, 667 x 375, 1280 x 800): the blocker is in the world geometry, not the camera.

- BEFORE: `docs/mobile/screenshots/fixes/F2-before.png` (avatar stuck at the tree line; the blue dashed rectangle is the old 48 px gap walk zone, ending left of the avatar)
- AFTER: `docs/mobile/screenshots/fixes/F2-after.png` (same walk, the avatar passes through the right half of the gap into the clearing; the gap zone now spans the whole opening)

## Root cause

`web/src/student/kingdom/town-scene.js`, line 42 (before the fix): `export const TOWN_GAP = { x: 386, w: 48 };`, used for the gap's walk zone at line 52 (`{ x: TOWN_GAP.x, y: -70, w: TOWN_GAP.w, h: 120 }`).

`canStand` (KingdomTown.vue) only lets the avatar's centre stand inside a walk zone. The town zone starts at y = 40 (the tree line), so north of y = 40 the only way out is the gap zone, x 386..434. But the forest is drawn by `makeEdgeTrees()` (same file, line 80), which leaves out every north-row tree within **50 px** of the trail (`inGap = x > GAP_X - 50 && x < GAP_X + 50`). The nearest trees left and right of the trail have their canopy edges at x = 373.9 and x = 474.2, so the visible opening is about **100 px wide (374..474)** while the walkable one was 48 px. The 40 px strip of open grass from x 434 to 474, right of the trail and directly above the BATTLES sign (433..503), was an invisible wall at y = 40. A player who walks up beside the sign (as the sign invites) hits it.

Not the cause (checked with the overlay and the BFS test): the tree beside the path (`TOWN_PROP_LAYOUT` tree at 432,140 is removed in favour of the sign, and the edge trees have no collider), the house (300..364, well left of the trail), the tile grid (cells at x 400 and 432 were open, so tap-to-move could already route through the gap), and the camera (the town/north zone switch at y = ±14 only moves the camera, it never blocks movement).

A second, smaller blocker sat at the same spot: the sign post collider (`KingdomTown.vue` line 72 before the fix, a circle r = 7 centred at y = 160) was nearly 3x the drawn 5 px post and 8 px above the post's foot (y = 168). An avatar at x ≈ 448 walking up was stopped at y ≈ 168 with about 6 px of visible grass between its feet and the post.

## Fix

- **Gap walk zone measured from the trees** (`town-scene.js`): `TOWN_GAP` is now computed by `measureGap()` from the two north-row edge trees flanking the trail (right canopy edge of the west tree to the left canopy edge of the east tree): x 374..474 (w 100). The trees, the trail and all drawings are unchanged (same seeded forest; the tree-skip rule now uses the named `GAP_CLEARANCE = 50`, the tree line the named `TOWN_ZONE_TOP = 40`).
- **Collision in one module** (`web/src/student/kingdom/town-collision.js`): the obstacle list, `canStandAt`, the walk grid, BFS (`searchFrom`) and `findPath`, moved out of `KingdomTown.vue` unchanged in behaviour so the reachability test runs on the real grid. Keys, the stick and tap-to-move all use the same `canStand` test; the tap-to-move grid is built from it (a cell is open when the avatar can stand at its centre). Sliding along colliders still works (the per-axis move in `tick()` is unchanged).
- **Props block only their base** (`config/tuning.js`, `TOWN_PROP_COLLIDER_RATIOS`, next to `TOWN_PROP_LAYOUT`): a share of each prop's layout box, centred on its bottom edge.
  - tree `{ w: 0.4, h: 0.2 }` (the brief's starting values; the canopy never blocks)
  - rock `{ w: 0.8, h: 0.35 }` (was a circle r = 15 that hung 3 px below the sprite and 8 px below the drawn rock)
  - house `{ w: 56/64, h: 1 }` (the drawn wall is 56 px wide; the 64 px footprint blocked 4 px of grass each side)
  - well `{ w: 36/50, h: 1 }` (the drawn basin is 36 px wide; the 50 px footprint blocked 7 px each side)
  - sign: `TOWN_SIGN_POST_RADIUS = 4` circle at the post's foot (was r = 7, 8 px too high)
  - barter corner, buildings, camps: unchanged (their footprints match the drawings; see audit)
- **Debug overlay** (`KingdomTown.vue`, `drawColliderDebug`): with `?debug=colliders` in the URL (comma lists allowed) the town draws the blocked pathfinding cells (red), the walk zones (blue dashed), every collider rectangle/circle (magenta), the door zones (yellow, `TOWN_DOOR_RADIUS`), the sprite boxes of the placed drawings (white dashed), the player's footprint and the current tap-to-move route (cyan). Without the parameter none of it runs (one boolean check per frame). The same gate exposes a read-only `window.__qqTown` handle (player, camera, view, doors, route, nearDoor) for the walk test; it is removed on unmount.

Desktop look is unchanged (no drawing changes outside the overlay). Desktop collision changes only where the colliders were larger than the drawings (the gap, the sign post, rocks, houses, the well, tree bases).

## Audit: every collider larger than its visual footprint

Found with the overlay (white sprite boxes vs magenta colliders) and checked by the unit test "every prop collider lies inside its drawing":

| Collider | Before | Drawing | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gap walk zone (north tree line) | x 386..434 | opening 374..474 | 374..474 (measured from the trees) |
| BATTLES sign post | circle r 7 at y 160 | 5 px post, foot at y 168 | circle r 4 at y 166 |
| Rocks (4) | circle r 15.1, bottom 3 px below the sprite | rock body 36 x 19, bottom 5 px above the sprite bottom | lower 35% x 80% of the sprite |
| Houses (12) | 64 x 40 footprint | wall 56 x 40 | 56 x 40, centred |
| Well | 50 x 22 footprint | basin 36 x 20 | 36 x 22, centred |
| Trees (3 in town) | 24% x 26% at 72..98% height | trunk 20% wide | 40% x 20% at the bottom centre (brief) |

Checked and left as they are: buildings (footprint = the level-0 scaffold and the built walls), Duel and Campfire camps (sign posts to pack/logs span the footprint), barter corner (the drawing is wider than its footprint), flowers (no collider), edge trees (decorative, outside the walk zones), the other walk-zone edges (town sides and bottom, clearing sides and top all sit under the edge trees' trunks or canopies; the BFS "open-looking ground" test finds no other invisible wall).

**Arena obstacles (audit only, not changed):** the arena has one solid obstacle, the campfire, a server-side circle r = 28 (`GAME_CONFIG.campfire.radius`, `src/hero-lab/room.js`). Its stone ring is drawn as an ellipse (about ±35 x ±21 px, `public/hero-lab/world.js` `drawCampfire`), so the circle reaches about 7..10 px above and below the drawn ring. It is collision, knockback and damage geometry on the server, so changing it would change gameplay balance (out of scope for this brief).

## Observations (not changed)

- On tall phones (412 x 915) the town camera centres the town zone, so the gap and the tree line (world y ≈ -70..50) sit under the kingdom header card and the resource chips while the avatar walks through it (about 0.35 s before the camera pans north at y = -14). On desktop 1280 x 800 the header card also covers the gap. The avatar is still moving there (the walk test checks it); the overlap belongs to F3 (header and chips).
- `player.moving` stays true (the hop plays) while the avatar pushes against a wall, because the per-axis move counts an unchanged axis as a move. Cosmetic; left alone.
- `config/tuning.js` still has old `TOWN_TRAILS` / `TOWN_WALK_ZONES` (gap at x 720) that nothing imports; `town-scene.js` defines the live ones. Left alone (dead config).

## Assumptions

- The worktree was created from `main`; it was moved to the `mobile-fixes` tip (a00488c) before any change, as the task says the work branches from `mobile-fixes`.
- `TOWN_PROP_COLLIDER_RATIOS` lives in `config/tuning.js`, next to `TOWN_PROP_LAYOUT`: it is the town config `KingdomTown.vue` already imports its `TOWN_*` numbers from, and the ratios apply on desktop too (they are not a mobile setting).
- The ratios are shares of each prop's layout box. For trees and rocks that box is the sprite (as the brief describes); for houses and the well it is the ground footprint the drawing stands on, so "base" means the drawn wall/basin there.
- "Northern end of the path" = the trail's fork (410, -232), both camp doors at the ends of its branches, and the clearing's top edge (y = -600) for the camera-clamp check.
- There was no visual debug gate in the town (the existing debug menu is the server-side demo menu), so the overlay uses `?debug=colliders`.

## Tests

- `web/src/student/kingdom/__tests__/town-collision.spec.js` (vitest, 13 tests): BFS on the real collision grid from the spawn: the spawn is walkable; all 7 doors are reachable and standable; every road/plaza/trail tile the avatar fits on is reachable; every open-looking tile connected to the spawn (not under the forest ring or a drawing, avatar fits; computed independently of the walk zones) is reachable; no walkable pocket is cut off; the gap spans the opening between the canopies; a straight walk up beside the sign (x = 446, y 230 → -60) is never blocked; prop colliders follow the ratios, lie inside the lower 60% of their drawings and never sit on a path. Run against the old 48 px gap it fails 3 tests and lists the unreachable open tiles (464, -32), (464, 0), (464, 32).
- `tests/mobile/town-walk.e2e.mjs` (Playwright, 5 tests; `npm run test:mobile -- town-walk`): drives real CDP touches on the town stick or the keyboard along the town's own route and fails if the avatar stops for more than 0.5 s while input is held: 412 x 915 stick, spawn → beside the sign → up through the gap → trail fork → Duel and Campfire doors → clearing top edge (camera clamp: avatar inside the canvas, camera steady while pushing) → all five town doors; 375 x 667 keys and 667 x 375 stick, up beside the sign into the clearing; 1280 x 800 keys, spawn → north end → all seven doors; 1280 x 800 diagonal sliding along the tree line and round a house. Run against the old gap it fails with "stalled at (437.7, 40.7)".
